Guidelines

BrandApplications

BrandApplications shows the logo at work on the business card, email signature and social profile, as reference layouts rather than a reusable component. **The consumer provides** the person's real name, title and contact lines; the preview shows placeholders and invents no one. **Business card** (3.5 x 2 in, 0.125 in bleed and safe margin): the front is full-bleed `ink-band` with the `ETA` wordmark top-left in `on-ink-band`, the reversed mark at two thirds of the card's width bleeding off the bottom-right corner (keep the apex and the meeting point of the faces on the card), and the domain bottom-left in `mono` `on-ink-band-muted`.

Guidelines

Button

Button is the action control in seven variants (`primary`, `secondary`, `outline`, `ghost`, `link`, `inverse`, `onDark`) and three heights (32, 40, 48px). **The consumer provides** a label from the fixed call-to-action vocabulary (Initiate Contact, Explore our services, Get started, Learn About ETA, Continue) and an `href` or click handler.

Guidelines

Badge

Badge is a small pill that tags an item with a short word, in three tones: `neutral`, `accent` and `gold`. **The consumer provides** the label text (one to three words) and the tone.

Guidelines

StatusChip

StatusChip shows the state of an item as a dot plus a word, in five tones: `neutral`, `info`, `success`, `pending` and `danger`. **The consumer provides** the status word and the tone.

Guidelines

StatTile

StatTile is a card that leads with one big numeral, then a label and a source line. **The consumer provides** the figure, a label that says what it counts, and a source.

Guidelines

ServiceCard

ServiceCard presents one of ETA's three offerings as a numbered card; the row is always exactly three, numbered 01 to 03. **The consumer provides** the number (01, 02 or 03), a short title and one sentence.

Guidelines

Eyebrow

Eyebrow is the small uppercase label that sits above a heading and names the section. **The consumer provides** one to four words.

Guidelines

SectionHeading

SectionHeading pairs an Eyebrow, an `h2` headline and an optional lead paragraph. **The consumer provides** the eyebrow words, a headline that makes a claim, and optionally one lead sentence.
