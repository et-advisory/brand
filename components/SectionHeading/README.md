# SectionHeading

SectionHeading pairs an Eyebrow, an `h2` headline and an optional lead paragraph.

**The consumer provides** the eyebrow words, a headline that makes a claim, and optionally one lead sentence. Markup is `.sh` containing `p.eyebrow`, `h2` and `p.lead`.

**When to use.** To open every page section or slide section. One per section.

**Do**
- Write the headline as an argument: lead with the claim, end with a period, sentence case.
- Keep the lead to one or two sentences in `ink-muted`.

**Do not**
- Do not use a headcount or a number as the hook of the header.
- Do not use the "X, not Y" construction in the headline.
- Do not set the headline in a color other than `ink`.
