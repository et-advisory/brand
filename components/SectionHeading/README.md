# SectionHeading

SectionHeading pairs an Eyebrow, an `h2` headline and an optional lead paragraph.

**The consumer provides** the eyebrow words, a headline written as one full thought about the reader, and optionally a lead. Render it as `<ETA.SectionHeading eyebrow="Capital formation" title="Raise capital on the asset you already own." lead="…" />`; `level` sets the heading tag (default `h2`) and `align="center"` centers it.

**When to use.** To open every page section or slide section. One per section.

**Do**
- Write the headline as one full thought about the reader, usually one sentence, ending with a period, in sentence case.
- Let the lead explain it in plain, conversational language: one or two sentences in `ink-muted`.

**Do not**
- Do not use a headcount or a number as the hook of the header.
- Do not use the "X, not Y" construction, "Not X. Y." fragments or stacked pairs of short declaratives in the headline.
- Do not set the headline in a color other than `ink`.
