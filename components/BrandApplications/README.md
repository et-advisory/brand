# BrandApplications

BrandApplications shows the logo at work on the business card, email signature and social profile, as reference layouts rather than a reusable component.

**The consumer provides** the person's real name, title and contact lines; the preview shows placeholders and invents no one.

**Business card** (3.5 x 2 in, 0.125 in bleed and safe margin): the front is full-bleed `ink-band` with the `ETA` wordmark top-left in `on-ink-band`, the reversed mark at two thirds of the card's width bleeding off the bottom-right corner (keep the apex and the meeting point of the faces on the card), and the domain bottom-left in `mono` `on-ink-band-muted`. The back is `card` on uncoated bright white: name in `ink` 800, title as an uppercase `accent-strong` eyebrow, the full-color mark at 34px top-right, and Email, Telephone and Address (the website) in `mono` on a `line` hairline, each label's initial in 900-weight `accent-strong` so the column spells E, T, A. No accent rule; the mark is the only color on the back.

**Email signature**: the mark at 44px, a `line` divider, name in `ink` 700, title and firm in `ink-muted`, the domain in `accent-strong` mono. Export the mark as PNG at 2x for mail clients that block SVG.

**Social**: the avatar is `eta-app-icon` or the reversed mark on `ink-band` in a circle, nudged up a few percent so its visual weight, which sits low in a triangle, lands on the circle's center. The banner is `ink-band` with one large cropped mark in `mark-reversed-deep` and a single sentence in `on-ink-band`.
