# BrandCanon

BrandCanon is the reference layout for Brand Evolution 13: the tagline plate and campaign line, the seven-swatch palette, the three working principles, the physical-to-digital flow, the four asset families and the three graphic-language drawings, built from tokens.

**The consumer provides** the real asset photography and map imagery; the preview draws no photographs and ships no stock.

**Tagline plate.** PHYSICAL ACTUALITY. DIGITAL POSSIBILITY. in wide-tracked 600-weight caps, `on-band-dark` on `band-dark` (Obsidian), radius `radius-md`. Once per surface, never inside a card.

**Campaign line.** A new market for the real world. in Inter 900, `ink`, tight tracking, with the supporting line in `ink-muted`. The period stays.

**Palette.** Ruby (`ruby`), Emerald (`accent`), Sapphire (`digital`), White (`white`), Obsidian (`band-dark`), Silver (`silver`), Gold (`gold-canon`). Emerald speaks for the interface, Sapphire marks the digital layer, Ruby stays in the mark; Silver and Gold are metals for thin linework.

**Principles.** Exactness, Totality, Alignment, in that order. Each has a number in `digital`, an uppercase title in `ink`, a one-line meaning in `ink-muted`, and a serif italic glyph in `ink-faint`, above a `line` rule.

**Flow.** Physical asset, Digital twin, Tokenized rights, Market participation, left to right on a `surface` panel. The physical and twin icons are `accent` line art; the rights and market icons are `digital`, with solid `digital` squares for tokens.

**Asset scope.** Land, Properties, Utilities, Resources: a bold uppercase label over its descriptor, under an `accent` rule.

**Graphic language.** Measure (a parcel polygon with measurement points over Sage contour lines), Represent (a parcel turning into a 3 by 3 grid), Connect (a phone showing the parcel, linked to person nodes).

**Do**
- Use thin strokes, `accent-tint` fills and Sage, Silver or Gold for map linework. The 13 board draws the token squares in gold; on White they stay `digital` here.
- Keep `digital` for tokens, rights and numerals only.
- Read the principles and flow steps in the order above.

**Do not**
- Do not add gradients or shadows to the drawings, and keep gold to thin linework, rules and token squares on Obsidian (`gold-canon` is 2.52:1 on White).
- Do not put Ruby anywhere but the mark.
- Do not use Sage as text on paper (it is 2.3:1).
