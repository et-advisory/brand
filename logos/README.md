# Logos

The ETA logo as adopted with Brand Evolution 12 (2026-10-14) and cut as stones in 3.1.0: Ruby left face, Sapphire right face, Emerald bottom face. Every SVG is outlined vector paths with no font dependency. In the full-color, reversed and gold marks each face is a cut stone: a lighter table in the middle and three bevels around it, the upper-left bevel lit and the other two in shade, with a soft shade across the stone toward the lower right. The one-color marks are flat. All SVGs come from `scripts/logo/generate.py`; the PNGs are exports of them for mail clients and Office.

- `eta-lockup-horizontal.svg` and `.png`: primary lockup for light grounds (faces #9b1738, #153e8a, #12664f; type #14151a).
- `eta-lockup-horizontal-reversed.svg` and `.png`: for band-dark (Obsidian, #111214) and dark photos (faces #f2748f, #7aa2f2, #52c4a0; type #f4f4f2). The PNG has a transparent ground.
- `eta-lockup-horizontal-mono-ink.svg`: one color, #14151a.
- `eta-lockup-stacked.svg`, `eta-lockup-stacked-reversed.svg` and their PNGs: centered and square spaces.
- `eta-wordmark.svg`: ETA with the descriptor, no mark, for running footers.
- `eta-mark.svg`, `eta-mark-reversed.svg` and their PNGs: the mark alone, full color.
- `eta-mark-mono-ink.svg` (#14151a) and `eta-mark-mono-white.svg` (#f4f4f2): one-color marks.
- `eta-mark-ceremonial-gold.svg`: gold faces (#f6d27a, #eeb32b, #a8761a) for band-dark only, never beside the three-color mark.
- `eta-app-icon.svg`, `eta-app-icon-512.png`: reversed mark on a #111214 rounded square (22% corner radius) for app icons, favicons and avatars.

Clear space is one third of the mark's height on every side. Minimum widths: horizontal lockup 160px, stacked 72px, mark 16px. The reversed faces are derived (lifted so each clears 3:1 on Obsidian); the full-color hexes are the canon swatches and form each stone's body, with the table and bevel tones mixed from them.
