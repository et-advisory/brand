# Logos

The ETA logo as adopted with Brand Evolution 12 (2026-10-14): Ruby left face, Sapphire right face, Emerald bottom face. Every SVG is outlined vector paths with no font dependency, and the full-color marks are faceted (three facets per face plus a sheen gradient, lit from the upper left); the PNGs are exports for mail clients and Office.

- `eta-lockup-horizontal.svg` and `.png`: primary lockup for light grounds (faces #9b1738, #153e8a, #12664f; type #14151a).
- `eta-lockup-horizontal-reversed.svg` and `.png`: for band-dark (Forest) and dark photos (faces #f2748f, #7aa2f2, #52c4a0; type #f4f4f2). The PNG has a transparent ground.
- `eta-lockup-horizontal-mono-ink.svg`: one color, #14151a.
- `eta-lockup-stacked.svg`, `eta-lockup-stacked-reversed.svg` and their PNGs: centered and square spaces.
- `eta-wordmark.svg`: ETA with the descriptor, no mark, for running footers.
- `eta-mark.svg`, `eta-mark-reversed.svg` and their PNGs: the mark alone, full color.
- `eta-mark-mono-ink.svg` (#14151a) and `eta-mark-mono-white.svg` (#ffffff): one-color marks.
- `eta-mark-ceremonial-gold.svg`: gold faces (#f6d27a, #eeb32b, #a8761a) for band-dark only, never beside the three-color mark.
- `eta-app-icon.svg`, `eta-app-icon-512.png`: reversed mark on a #14151a rounded square (22% corner radius) for app icons, favicons and avatars.

Clear space is one third of the mark's height on every side. Minimum widths: horizontal lockup 160px, stacked 72px, mark 16px. The reversed faces are derived (lifted so each clears 3:1 on Forest); the full-color hexes are the canon swatches and form each face's middle tone, with the facet shades derived from them.
