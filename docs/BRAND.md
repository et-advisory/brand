ET Advisory (ETA) helps founders and asset owners close capital raises, with a focus on real-world asset tokenization. The look is confident, bright and precise: warm paper, near-black ink, Emerald as the brand voice, Forest as the dark ground, heavy Inter headlines, and a three-color mark. Tokenization is the instrument; the deliverable is a closed raise or a growth-exit strategy, and the design should read that way.

**Brand Evolution 12 is the canon (adopted 2026-10-14, "as canon as it gets for now").** It replaces Brand Evolution 06 and the Royal Blue direction. The board is in the Boards asset group (`eta-brand-evolution-12.png`); the section below records it in full and the rest of this book says how to build with it.

The dark-theme values, `accent-strong`, `accent-tint` and the lifted faces of the reversed mark are derived (the board states only the six hexes) and flagged in their token notes.

**Typeface.** Inter for headlines, body and data. The `mono` family now resolves to Inter, so figures and identifiers set in Inter with tabular numerals. `Instrument Serif` stays only inside the mark's lineage and for the three Greek glyphs of the principles.

**Working principles.** Three, always in this order, each with a Greek glyph in `ink-faint` (italic serif), a number in `digital` and a one-line meaning.

1. **Exactness** (ε). Ground digital records in physical facts.
2. **Totality** (τ). See the asset, its rights and its wider impact. τ = 2π.
3. **Alignment** (α). Connect owners, operators and long-term stewardship.

**Zoom, from world to rights.** The hero image zooms in four labeled steps: WORLD (globe), REGION (aerial of the area), ASSET LOCATION (boundary polygon, contour lines and a location pin on the map), DIGITAL RIGHTS (the document-and-grid icon). Labels are small caps joined by hairline leaders; a north arrow and scale bar sit on the map.

## Principles

- **Emerald leads, Sapphire is the digital layer.** `accent` (Emerald) is the brand voice on marketing and proposal surfaces: fills, buttons, big numerals and links at 24px and up; text under 24px uses `accent-strong`. `digital` (Sapphire) marks only the digital side of the story: tokens, rights and the principle numerals. Never use both as large fills on one surface.
- **Ruby lives in the mark.** `ruby` and the left face of the mark are the only places Ruby appears. It is not a UI, status or text color.
- **Gold is a different brand.** `gold` appears only for bullion and ceremonial moments (the gold Badge tone, the ceremonial gold mark on `band-dark`); text on a gold fill is `on-gold`. Do not place it beside the three-color mark.
- **Paper, not white.** Backgrounds are `paper`; cards are `card`; bands are `surface`; rules are `line`; dark grounds are `band-dark`. Always set a section background explicitly.
- **Seven module hues belong to ERA.** Do not import them into client-facing ETA collateral.
- **Legacy.** `letter-e`, `letter-t` and `letter-a` belong to the retired letter-colored wordmark; the mark does not use them. Keep them only for older material.

## Content fundamentals

- Sentence case, a period on headlines, no emoji, no exclamation marks, no first-person singular. Em dashes are allowed in the source voice; this team avoids them in deck copy.
- Fixed calls to action: Initiate Contact, Explore our services, Get started, Learn About ETA, Continue.
- Lead with the claim, then the proof. A header is an argument, not a headcount.
- Short declaratives in pairs, as the canon does: the physical world first, its digital chapter second.

## Visual foundations

- **Type.** `Inter` for everything (800 to 900 on headlines, 600 on labels, 400 and 500 for body and data). `Instrument Serif` appears only for the principle glyphs. The `mono` token stays in the file as the data role and resolves to Inter.
- **Slides.** 1920x1080, `margin-slide` 128px, eyebrow in `slide-label` uppercase with wide tracking, `slide-cover` or `slide-title` headline, 24px footer row at 64px from the bottom in `ink-faint`.
- **Statement slides** use `band-dark` (Forest) as the ground with `on-band-dark` type and the reversed mark. One per deck section at most.
- **Radius.** `radius-lg` cards, `radius-md` buttons, `radius-pill` badges and tags.
- **Photography.** People: only the four real team photos (Abi, Erik, Ryan, Tyler), square, `object-fit: cover`, hairline border. Assets: land, properties, utilities and resources, photographed from above or in landscape and paired with map linework (parcel boundaries, contour lines, a location pin). No other stock imagery.
- **Open decisions.** Base spacing unit (4px here, 8px in the Style Guide). The business card, banner and booklet on the board are direction, not final art. Whether the Sage wireframe weight needs a token.

## Logo (Brand Evolution 12, adopted 2026-10-14)

**The idea.** A faceted crystal seen from above: three faces meeting at one apex, in Ruby (left), Sapphire (right) and Emerald (bottom). Each face is cut into three facets, a lit one, a shaded one and a deep one, with a faint sheen across the face, so it reads as a polished stone lit from the upper left. The canon swatch is the face's middle tone; the facets are shifts of it. The one-color marks stay flat. The three faces are the asset, the instrument and the raise folded into one solid, and the apex is the close. The faces are separated by a narrow channel, so the mark still reads in one color, at 16px and on a fax.

**Parts.** The mark; the `ETA` wordmark, drawn geometric caps matched to Inter at 800 to 900 (paths, not live type); and the descriptor `ET ADVISORY`, monoline caps tracked to the exact width of the wordmark. All three are outlined vectors, so no file depends on a font.

**Lockups.** Horizontal is primary: mark on the left, `ETA` top-aligned to the upper third, descriptor on the mark's baseline. Stacked is for square and centered spaces (business card front, title slides). The mark alone is for app icons, favicons, avatars and anything under 160px wide.

**Color.**
- On `paper`, `surface` or `card`: faces `mark-facet-light` (Ruby), `mark-facet-mid` (Sapphire), `mark-facet-deep` (Emerald); type in `ink`.
- On `band-dark` (Forest) or a dark photo: faces `mark-reversed-light`, `-mid`, `-deep` (lifted Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald so each clears 3:1 on Forest); type in `on-band-dark`.
- One color: all `ink`, all `on-band-dark`, or all `on-accent` on an `accent` fill. The channels carry the form.
- Ceremonial: `gold-light`, `gold`, `gold-deep` on `band-dark` only, for bullion clients and seals. Never beside the three-color mark.

**Clear space and size.** Clear space x = one third of the mark's height on every side. Minimum: horizontal lockup 160px or 40mm wide, stacked 72px, mark 16px (one color under 24px).

**Do not** flatten the full-color mark, change the lighting direction, recolor the faces (not with other palette hues, not gold), swap the face order, rotate or flip it, stretch it, add shadows, outlines or gradients, close the channels, set the name in live type beside the mark, or place the full-color mark on `accent` or `band-dark` (use the reversed one).

**Files** are in the Logos asset group: SVG for every lockup and mark (`eta-lockup-horizontal`, `-reversed`, `-mono-ink`; `eta-lockup-stacked`, `-reversed`; `eta-wordmark`; `eta-mark`, `-reversed`, `-mono-ink`, `-mono-white`, `-ceremonial-gold`; `eta-app-icon`), plus PNG exports of the color and reversed lockups and marks for mail clients and Office, and the 512px app icon. Use SVG everywhere a vector is accepted. The Boards group holds the Evolution 12 board as a reference picture only; never lift the logo from it.

## Iconography

The source system uses Lucide (0.474.0, stroke 2, `currentColor`) through an `Icon` component. No icon files ship in this system; add Lucide icons at `currentColor` when needed. The canon graphic language (measure, represent, connect, and the four flow icons Asset, Twin, Rights, Market) is drawn as thin line art: `accent` strokes for the physical side, `digital` for rights and market, `accent-tint` fills and solid `digital` squares for tokens. `BrandCanon` shows each drawing. Use no emoji.
