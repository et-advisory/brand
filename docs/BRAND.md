ET Advisory (ETA) helps founders and asset owners close capital raises, with a focus on real-world asset tokenization. The look is confident, bright and material: white marble and warm white grounds, obsidian as the dark ground, silver and gold as the metals, heavy Inter headlines, a three-gem mark (Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald) and photography of stone, metal, glass and land. Emerald stays the interface accent. Tokenization is the instrument; the deliverable is a closed raise or a growth-exit strategy, and the design should read that way.

**Brand Evolution 13 is the canon (supplied by Erik Hanzlick on 2026-10-14).** It replaces Brand Evolution 12, which had replaced 06 and the Royal Blue direction. The positioning, copy, working principles, flow, asset scope, graphic language and the three-gem mark carry over unchanged. Evolution 13 changes the palette (White, Obsidian, Silver and Gold join the three gems, and Obsidian returns as the dark ground), adds a stone-and-metal material language with a photography direction, and redraws the applications. The Boards asset group holds the Evolution 13 board and four reference sheets; Evolution 12 and 06 stay there for the record. Where this book and a board differ, this book and `tokens.json` win.

**Working principles.** Three, always in this order, each with a Greek glyph in `ink-faint` (italic serif), a number and a one-line meaning. The board sets the numerals 01 to 03 in gold; keep them `digital` on White, where gold fails contrast.

1. **Exactness** (ε). Ground digital records in physical facts.
2. **Totality** (τ). See the asset, its rights and its wider impact. τ = 2π.
3. **Alignment** (α). Connect owners, operators and long-term stewardship.

## Principles

- **Emerald leads, Sapphire is the digital layer.** `accent` (Emerald) is the brand voice on marketing and proposal surfaces: fills, buttons, big numerals and links at 24px and up; text under 24px uses `accent-strong`. `digital` (Sapphire) marks the digital side of the story: rights icons and the right face of the mark. The 13 board sets token squares and principle numerals in gold; `gold-canon` is 2.52:1 on White, so on White keep them `digital` (or `ink`), and use `gold-canon` for them on Obsidian (6.94:1) and at 24px and up. Never use both as large fills on one surface.
- **Ruby lives in the mark.** `ruby` and the left face of the mark are the only places Ruby appears. It is not a UI, status or text color.
- **Gold is a canon metal.** `gold-canon` (#B79A58) draws linework, rules, frames, token squares and principle numerals on White and Obsidian, always thin or small: never a large fill and never behind text. The bright bullion gold (`gold` #EEB32B, the gold Badge tone and the ceremonial mark on `band-dark`) stays for bullion and ceremonial moments only, and text on a gold fill is `on-gold`. Etalon Vault keeps its own gold-and-dark brand; do not borrow its art.
- **Paper or White, set explicitly.** Backgrounds are `paper` (#FBFAF9, the Evolution 12 ground) or the canon `white` (#F7F7F4); cards are `card`; bands are `surface`; rules are `line`; dark grounds are `band-dark` (Obsidian). Always set a section background explicitly.
- **Seven module hues belong to ERA.** Do not import them into client-facing ETA collateral.
- **Legacy.** `letter-e`, `letter-t` and `letter-a` belong to the retired letter-colored wordmark; the mark does not use them. Keep them only for older material.

## Content fundamentals

- Sentence case, a period on headlines, no emoji, no exclamation marks, no first-person singular. Em dashes are allowed in the source voice; this team avoids them in deck copy.
- Fixed calls to action: Initiate Contact, Explore our services, Get started, Learn About ETA, Continue.
- Lead with the claim, then the proof. A header is an argument, not a headcount.
- Short declaratives in pairs, as the canon does: the physical world first, its digital chapter second.

## Visual foundations

- **Type.** `Inter` for everything (800 to 900 on headlines, 600 on labels, 400 and 500 for body and data). `Instrument Serif` appears only for the principle glyphs. The `mono` token stays in the file as the data role and resolves to Inter.
- **Slides.** 1920x1080, `margin-slide` 128px, eyebrow in `slide-label` uppercase with wide tracking, `slide-cover` or `slide-title` headline, 24px footer row at 64px from the bottom in `ink-faint`.
- **Statement slides** use `band-dark` (Obsidian) as the ground with `on-band-dark` type and the reversed mark. One per deck section at most.
- **Radius.** `radius-lg` cards, `radius-md` buttons, `radius-pill` badges and tags.
- **Photography.** People: only the four real team photos (Abi, Erik, Ryan, Tyler), square, `object-fit: cover`, hairline border. Assets: land, properties, utilities and resources, photographed from above or in landscape and paired with map linework (parcel boundaries, contour lines, a location pin). No other stock imagery.
- **Scene grounds.** Four licence-free aerials in `assets/scenes/` (land, properties, resources, utilities; credits and licences in `assets/scenes/CREDITS.md`) are the photographic grounds behind the site's story scenes. One treatment for all: `saturate(.45) brightness(.6)`, an Obsidian gradient solid behind the copy column, the grid field on top, no grain. Line drawings sit over them, never under text.
- **Open decisions.** Base spacing unit (4px here, 8px in the Style Guide). The business card, banner and booklet on the board are direction, not final art. Whether Emerald stays the interface accent now that the 13 board sets numerals, rules and token squares in gold. Whether Forest, Sage and Paper are retired. The tagline wording (the board prints value, Erik's ruling is actuality). Whether the silver, obsidian and wireframe marks are drawn.

## Logo (adopted with Brand Evolution 12 on 2026-10-14, carried into 13; geometry corrected 2026-10-17)

**The idea.** Three cut stones forming one tetrahedron, seen from the front and above: an upright equilateral silhouette with the apex on the centreline above the centroid, so the solid reads as a true tetrahedron rather than a tilted crystal. The left stone is Ruby, the right Sapphire, the front Emerald. Each stone has a lighter table in the middle and three bevels around it, the upper-left bevel lit and the other two in shade, with a highlight sweeping from the lit corner across the table, so it reads as a polished gem lit from the upper left. The canon swatch is the stone's body; the bevels shift it. The one-color marks stay flat. The three stones are the asset, the instrument and the raise meeting at one apex, which is the close. Channels of six units separate the stones, so the mark still reads in one color, at 16px and on a fax. Geometry and shading come from `scripts/logo/generate.py`.

**Parts.** The mark; the `ETA` wordmark, drawn geometric caps matched to Inter at 800 to 900 (paths, not live type); and the descriptor `ET ADVISORY`, monoline caps tracked to the exact width of the wordmark. All three are outlined vectors, so no file depends on a font.

**Lockups.** Horizontal is primary: mark on the left, `ETA` top-aligned to the upper third, descriptor on the mark's baseline. Stacked is for square and centered spaces (business card front, title slides). The mark alone is for app icons, favicons, avatars and anything under 160px wide.

**Color.**
- On `paper`, `surface` or `card`: faces `mark-facet-light` (Ruby), `mark-facet-mid` (Sapphire), `mark-facet-deep` (Emerald); type in `ink`.
- On `band-dark` (Obsidian) or a dark photo: faces `mark-reversed-light`, `-mid`, `-deep` (lifted Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald so each clears 3:1 on a dark ground); type in `on-band-dark`.
- One color: all `ink`, all `on-band-dark`, or all `on-accent` on an `accent` fill. The channels carry the form.
- Ceremonial: `gold-light`, `gold`, `gold-deep` on `band-dark` only, for bullion clients and seals. Never beside the three-color mark.

**Clear space and size.** Clear space x = one third of the mark's height on every side. Minimum: horizontal lockup 160px or 40mm wide, stacked 72px, mark 16px (one color under 24px).

**Do not** flatten the full-color mark, change the lighting direction, recolor the faces (not with other palette hues, not gold), swap the face order, rotate or flip it, stretch it, add shadows, outlines or gradients, close the channels, set the name in live type beside the mark, or place the full-color mark on `accent` or `band-dark` (use the reversed one).

**Files** are in the Logos asset group: SVG for every lockup and mark (`eta-lockup-horizontal`, `-reversed`, `-mono-ink`; `eta-lockup-stacked`, `-reversed`; `eta-wordmark`; `eta-mark`, `-reversed`, `-mono-ink`, `-mono-white`, `-ceremonial-gold`; `eta-app-icon`), plus PNG exports of the color and reversed lockups and marks for mail clients and Office, and the 512px app icon. Use SVG everywhere a vector is accepted. The Boards group holds the Evolution 12 board as a reference picture only; never lift the logo from it.

## Iconography

The source system uses Lucide (0.474.0, stroke 2, `currentColor`) through an `Icon` component. No icon files ship in this system; add Lucide icons at `currentColor` when needed. The canon graphic language (measure, represent, connect, and the four flow icons Asset, Twin, Rights, Market) is drawn as thin line art: `accent` strokes for the physical side, `digital` for rights and market, `accent-tint` fills and solid `digital` squares for tokens. `BrandCanon` shows each drawing. Use no emoji.

The site's scene drawings extend this language; see the site's `docs/story/VISUALS.md`.
